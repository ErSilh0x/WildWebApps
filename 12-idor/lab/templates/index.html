▸ Training notes - Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) · A01:2025 · click to expand

Your profile is addressed by your account id in the URL (GET /profile?id=1003), and the server looks the account up by that number with no check that it belongs to you. There is no account picker on the page: you tamper the id directly in the address bar, or with an intercepting proxy like Burp Suite. Account ids are plain sequential numbers, so walking the id to the administrator (account 1001) reveals their private account recovery key, the value you submit below.
