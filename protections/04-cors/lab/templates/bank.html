  • /api/no-cors - no CORS headers. The Same-Origin Policy blocks any other origin from reading it.

  • /api/exact - exact allowlist naming {{ partner_origin }} (not your console).
  • /api/preflight - needs a custom header, so it is preflighted (OPTIONS), and exposes a header.
  • /api/null-origin - trusts the literal null origin with credentials (a misconfiguration).
  • /api/profile - the admin's API key, behind a broken origin check. This is the challenge.
